Privacy Policy — Bin Do Magic

Effective date: September 24, 2026 · Developer: Beijing Xianyuandu Technology Co., Ltd. (北京仙缘渡科技有限公司)

Summary

Bin Do Magic is an offline-first personal collection app. All of your content stays on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No account or registration — nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, statistics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, or device identifiers.

Where your data lives

All items you store (text, images, audio, video, files) are saved in the app's own storage directory on your device and never leave it unless you explicitly share or export them yourself (for example, sharing an item to another app, or exporting a zip backup).

Purchases

The optional Premium subscription is processed entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing / Google Play Payments). We never see your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on any device with that account via the in-app "Restore purchase" button. For details on how Google handles purchase data, see Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only by Google Play Billing for the optional subscription and restore flow. The app itself works fully offline.
  • Access to files, images, audio and video happens only through the system share sheet or the file picker you interact with, for the content you choose to add.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions or requests: xianyuandu@magicdo.top

隐私政策 — 小仙收纳盒（Bin Do Magic）

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日 · 开发者：北京仙缘渡科技有限公司

摘要

小仙收纳盒是一款离线优先的个人收纳应用。您的全部内容都保存在您自己的设备上。我们不收集、不传输、不共享您的任何个人数据。

我们不收集的数据

  • 无账号、无注册，无需提供任何身份信息。
  • 无统计、分析或追踪 SDK。
  • 无广告 SDK。
  • 不访问您的通讯录、位置信息或设备标识符。

您的数据存储在哪里

您存储的所有内容（文字、图片、音频、视频、文件）保存在您设备上本应用专属的存储目录中，除非您主动分享或导出（例如分享给其他应用、导出 zip 备份），否则不会离开您的设备。

购买

可选的高级会员订阅完全由 Google Play（Google Play 结算 / Google Play 付款）处理，我们无法接触您的付款信息。购买绑定您的 Google 账号，可在任意设备上通过应用内"恢复购买"找回。Google 如何处理购买数据请参见 Google 的隐私政策。

权限说明

  • 网络：仅用于 Google Play 结算服务的可选订阅与恢复购买流程；应用本体完全离线可用。
  • 对文件、图片、音视频的访问，均通过系统分享面板或您主动操作的文件选择器进行，且仅限您选择添加的内容。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

变更与联系

政策如有变更，将在本页面更新。疑问或请求请联系：xianyuandu@magicdo.top
