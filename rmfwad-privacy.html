Privacy Policy for Rich Man 3K

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Introduction

Rich Man 3K ("the App") is a single-player turn-based strategy game developed and published by Beijing Xianyandu Technology Co., Ltd. (北京仙缘渡科技有限公司). This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use our App.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit personal information
  • Require user registration or accounts
  • Collect, store, or transmit personal information on our side (in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play — any related network communication is handled by Google, and we never receive your payment details)
  • Use analytics services or advertising SDKs
  • Share any data with third parties

Local Data Storage

The App stores game save data locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data includes:

  • Game progress and save files
  • App settings (language, sound preferences)

This data remains on your device only and is never transmitted to any server. Uninstalling the App will delete all local data.

Permissions

The App does not request any device permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device features.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from children or any other users.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks that collect data.
